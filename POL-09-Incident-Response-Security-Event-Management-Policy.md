# INCIDENT RESPONSE & SECURITY EVENT MANAGEMENT POLICY

## 1.0 PURPOSE

This policy establishes a structured process for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents at ______ (Enter your Company Name). It ensures timely containment, minimal business impact, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement.

---

## 2.0 SCOPE

This policy applies to all security incidents, data breaches, operational disruptions, and suspected security events affecting systems, data, staff, or third-party relationships at ______ (Enter your Company Name).

---

## 3.0 POLICY STATEMENTS

### 3.1 Incident Response Lifecycle

______ (Enter your Company Name) follows a six-phase incident response lifecycle:

| Phase | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Preparation** | The CISO maintains the Incident Response Plan and trains the SIRT. |
| 2 | **Detection and Analysis** | Security Operations monitors alerts and declares incidents. |
| 3 | **Containment** | The SIRT isolates affected systems. |
| 4 | **Eradication** | IT removes threats and patches vulnerabilities. |
| 5 | **Recovery** | IT restores systems from clean backups. |
| 6 | **Lessons Learned** | The CISO conducts a post-incident review within five business days. |

---

### 3.2 Incident Severity Classification

| Severity | Description | Response SLA |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 – Critical** | Confirmed data breach; ransomware; system-wide outage | Contain within 1 hour; notify CISO and CEO immediately |
| **P2 – High** | Suspected compromise; major service degradation | Contain within 4 hours; notify CISO within 1 hour |
| **P3 – Medium** | Localised security event | Respond within 24 hours |
| **P4 – Low** | Minor policy violation | Respond within 72 hours |

---

### 3.3 Security Incident Response Team (SIRT)

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Incident Commander** (______ Enter CISO or delegate name) | Overall incident management and escalation |
| **Technical Lead** (______ Enter Security Engineer name) | Investigation, containment, and evidence preservation |
| **IT Operations Lead** (______ Enter IT Lead name) | System isolation and backup restoration |
| **Communications Lead** (______ Enter Legal/PR name) | Internal and external communications |
| **Legal Advisor** (______ Enter Legal/Compliance name) | Regulatory obligations and legal hold |

---

### 3.4 Breach Notification

______ (Enter your Company Name) will notify the supervisory authority within **72 hours** of a data breach. Affected clients will be notified as per contract terms. Affected individuals will be notified if the breach poses high risk. All notifications are documented in a breach register.

---

### 3.5 Security Event Monitoring (SIEM)

______ (Enter your Company Name) deploys an enterprise SIEM solution with the following characteristics:

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Log Sources** | Firewalls, IDS/IPS, Kaspersky endpoints, servers, authentication systems, and applications |
| **Log Retention (Hot)** | 12 months |
| **Log Retention (Archive)** | 6 years |
| **SIEM Rule Tuning** | Quarterly |
| **Critical / High Alert Monitoring** | 24/7 |

---

### 3.6 IR Plan Testing

______ (Enter your Company Name) conducts the following testing activities:

| Activity | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Tabletop exercise with the SIRT | Annually |
| Technical simulated incident exercise | Annually |

Results are documented and improvement actions are tracked. The IR Plan is updated after each exercise and after real incidents.

---

## 4.0 RESPONSIBILITIES

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **CISO** (______ Enter CISO Name) | Owns this policy, maintains the IR Plan, and makes regulatory notification decisions. |
| **Security Operations** | Performs 24/7 SIEM monitoring, initial triage, and incident declaration. |
| **SIRT Members** | Execute the IR Plan per their assigned roles. |
| **IT Operations** | Handles technical recovery, system isolation, and backup restoration. |
| **HR** | Supports staff-related incidents and insider threat investigations. |
| **Legal and Compliance** | Manages regulatory notification, legal hold, and evidence. |
| **All Staff** | Must report suspicious activity immediately and cooperate with investigations. |
